0 · Basealways on Cmd · Alt · Enter under the left thumb, Space · Backspace · Ctrl under the right. Orange-edged keys open the other layers. Esc, Caps Lock, both Shifts, Backspace and ' on the outer columns.
1 · NavHOLD Nav: left upper inner key, left index Screenshots: Nav + W selected area; Nav + E toolbar (Cmd+Shift+5), then choose Capture Selected Window; Nav + R full screen. Release Nav after tapping. Arrows: H J K L; word jumps: Y O; Home PgDn PgUp End below. Nav + Space turns mouse mode on. Nav + Backspace thumb locks numbers on; Backspace stays on the outer top-right key.
2 · Num + SymHOLD the Backspace thumb  ·  or LOCK: Nav + Backspace thumb, tap the Backspace thumb to unlock  ·  or HOLD Alt+Num (right lower inner key) = same keys with Alt held Hold Backspace, tap the Fn-position key: != (no spaces). Use plain NumSym, not Alt+Num, for code. Left: numpad, 0 on A, + . - inner; £ on Nav (Right Option+3), $ on Tab. Right: { } ( ) [ ] top to bottom; ! : & inner, # = * ring, _ | @ pinky, ^ on the outer home key. Alt+3 is now free; workspace 7 uses Alt+Num + W.
3 · FnHOLD Fn: right upper inner key F1-F9 in the numpad grid, F10 F11 F12 down the inner column. Vol- Vol+ on Y U, Prev Next on I O, Play Mute on K L. Fn + Esc swaps Ctrl and Cmd for Linux.
4 · MouseNav + Space = ON  ·  Esc or Nav = OFF Pointer on H J K L, wheel on N M , . (M pulls the page toward you). Thumbs: Space left click, Backspace right click, Ctrl middle click. Hold Space and move to drag. Left hand still types; Cmd+click and Shift+click work.
